\section{Result Comparison}
\label{sec:results}

After configuration selection, every chosen model was refitted on the complete clean 
training corpus and evaluated on the untouched official test set. 
Table~\ref{tab:test-results} and Figure~\ref{fig:model-comparison} provide the direct 
traditional-versus-modern comparison.

\subsection{Overall Test Performance}

The TF-IDF linear SVM achieved the highest test macro-F1, 0.5812. 
TF-IDF Logistic Regression had slightly higher accuracy (0.5687) but lower 
macro-F1 (0.5727). Both GloVe classifiers were substantially weaker: Logistic 
Regression reached 0.4223 accuracy and 0.4275 macro-F1, while linear SVM reached 0.4176 
accuracy and 0.4258 macro-F1. Validation and test rankings were consistent.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4.5pt}
\begin{tabular}{|l|r|r|r|r|r|}
\hline
Model & Accuracy & Macro P & Macro R & Macro-F1 & Weighted F1 \\
\hline
TF-IDF + Linear SVM & 0.5674 & 0.5808 & 0.5830 & \textbf{0.5812} & 0.5666 \\
\hline
TF-IDF + Logistic Regression & \textbf{0.5687} & 0.6001 & 0.5624 & 0.5727 & 0.5679 \\
\hline
GloVe + Logistic Regression & 0.4223 & 0.4701 & 0.4122 & 0.4275 & 0.4239 \\
\hline
GloVe + Linear SVM & 0.4176 & 0.4442 & 0.4183 & 0.4258 & 0.4188 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Official held-out test-set performance. Best accuracy and macro-F1 are emphasized.}

\end{table}

\subsection{Class-Level Performance of the Best Model}

The selected TF-IDF linear SVM performed best on Neutral tweets (F1 0.6703) and Extremely Positive tweets (F1 0.6254). Its lowest class F1 was 0.5063 for Negative tweets. The gap among classes confirms why the macro average is more informative than accuracy alone.

\subsection{GloVe Selected Model on the Test Set}

The validation-selected GloVe Logistic Regression pipeline was refitted on all 41,141 clean training rows and then evaluated on all 3,798 official test rows. It achieved 0.4223 accuracy and 0.4275 macro-F1. Its strongest class was Neutral (F1 0.4778), while Positive had the lowest F1 (0.4070). Table~\ref{tab:glove-class-results} and Figure~\ref{fig:glove-confusion} provide the class-level test evidence that was previously missing from the report.
